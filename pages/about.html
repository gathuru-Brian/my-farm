About Kuria Farms

Our Story

Kuria Farms is dedicated to providing quality agricultural products while embracing sustainable farming practices. Our mission is to improve food security, create employment, and support local communities through modern agriculture.

We specialize in crop production, dairy farming, poultry farming, and agricultural consultancy services. Through innovation and technology, we aim to become a leading agribusiness enterprise in Kenya and beyond.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality agricultural products while promoting sustainable farming and community development.

Our Vision

To be a leading agricultural enterprise recognized for innovation, sustainability, and excellence.
